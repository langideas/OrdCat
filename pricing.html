怎么开通How to subscribe

在哪里开通会员？Where do I subscribe?

打开 OrdCat → 设置 → 用户，登录后在「会员」里选月付或年付，点「开通 Pro」或「开通 Max」。会打开 Stripe 的付款页，付完回到 App 几秒后就生效。Open OrdCat → Settings → User, sign in, choose monthly or yearly under Membership and click Get Pro or Get Max. Stripe's checkout page opens in your browser; back in the app it switches on within seconds.

怎么取消或者换卡？How do I cancel or change my card?

设置 → 用户 → 管理会员，会打开 Stripe 的管理页面，可以取消、换付款方式、下载发票。取消后当期结束前照常使用。Settings → User → Manage membership opens Stripe's billing page, where you can cancel, change your payment method and download invoices. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

支持哪些付款方式？Which payment methods work?

银行卡（Visa、Mastercard、American Express），以及 Stripe 在你所在地区提供的其他方式。Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and other methods Stripe offers in your region.

我的对话会被保存吗？Are my conversations stored?

不会。托管模型只转发这一次的请求，只记录用了多少条额度。聊天记录和记忆都只存在你的 Mac 上，详见 隐私政策。No. Hosted models only relay each request and we only record how many messages you used. Chats and memory stay on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.
