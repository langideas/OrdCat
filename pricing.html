怎么开通How to subscribe

在哪里开通会员？Where do I subscribe?

打开 OrdCat → 设置 → 用户，登录后在「会员」里选月付或年付，点 Pro 或 Max 卡片上的「开通」。浏览器会打开 Stripe 的付款页，付完会看到结算单，回到 App 几秒后自动生效，同时你会收到确认邮件。Open OrdCat → Settings → User, sign in, choose monthly or yearly under Membership and click Get Pro or Get Max on the card. Stripe's checkout opens in your browser; after paying you see a receipt, the plan switches on in the app within seconds, and a confirmation email arrives.

怎么升级或降级？How do I upgrade or downgrade?

在同一个地方点 Max 卡片上的「升级」（或 Pro 卡片上的「降级」、「改为年付」）。会打开 Stripe 的确认页，不会重复订阅：升级马上生效，差价按当期剩余天数折算；降级在当期结束时生效，之前照常使用。In the same place, click Upgrade on the Max card (or Downgrade / Switch to yearly on Pro). Stripe's confirmation page opens; you never end up with two subscriptions. Upgrades apply right away and the difference is prorated for the rest of the period; downgrades apply at the end of the period.

怎么取消？取消后会怎样？How do I cancel, and what happens then?

设置 → 用户 → 管理订阅，或者不用登录直接用 Verträge hier kündigen。取消后不再续费，当期结束前照常使用，到期自动回到免费版；结束前随时可以恢复订阅。我们会发邮件确认。Settings → User → Manage subscription, or without signing in via Verträge hier kündigen. It won't renew, you keep everything until the end of the paid period and then go back to Free. You can resume any time before it ends. We confirm by email.

能退款吗？Can I get a refund?

会员和技能订阅：购买后 14 天内可以撤回，按已使用的天数付费，其余退回原付款方式。小鱼干和加油包是数字内容，到账后就不能撤回（付款时你会勾选同意）。详见 撤回说明，在线撤回点 Vertrag widerrufen。之后的期间不按天退款，取消后用到当期结束。Plans and skills: you can withdraw within 14 days of buying and pay only for the days used; the rest goes back to your payment method. Dried Fish and top-ups are digital content and can't be withdrawn once credited (you agree to this at checkout). See the withdrawal information, or withdraw online via Vertrag widerrufen. Later periods aren't refunded by the day; after cancelling you keep access until the period ends.

扣款失败怎么办？What if a payment fails?

Stripe 会在几天内自动重试，这期间会员照常可用，App 里会提示「更新付款方式」，我们也会发邮件。一直失败的话订阅结束，回到免费版。Stripe retries automatically over a few days and your plan keeps working. The app shows Update payment method and we email you. If every retry fails, the subscription ends and you go back to Free.

支持哪些付款方式？发票在哪里？Which payment methods work? Where are invoices?

银行卡（Visa、Mastercard、American Express），以及 Stripe 在你所在地区提供的其他方式。付完的结算单页面可以打印或存 PDF；订阅的发票在 设置 → 用户 → 管理订阅 里下载。Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and other methods Stripe offers in your region. You can print or save the receipt page after paying; subscription invoices are in Settings → User → Manage subscription.

我的对话会被保存吗？Are my conversations stored?

不会。托管模型只转发这一次的请求，只记录用了多少条额度。聊天记录和记忆都只存在你的 Mac 上，详见 隐私政策。No. Hosted models only relay each request and we only record how many messages you used. Chats and memory stay on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.
